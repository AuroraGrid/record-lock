# Security Policy

This policy applies to the sanitized public reference repository. Production deployments may use additional controls that are intentionally not documented here.

Do not disclose active vulnerabilities, credentials, private personal information, restricted source files, sealed records, or unlawful content through public issues.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner and include the affected record or component, a concise description, safe reproduction steps, potential impact, and whether analytical integrity or record history can be altered.

Urgent reports include exposed credentials, unauthorized access to restricted source packages, modification of released records without a revision event, review-gate bypasses, source-identifier collisions, broken provenance links, injection vulnerabilities, and public export of restricted fields.

Testing must avoid privacy harm, data destruction, service disruption, credential access, social engineering, and retrieval or redistribution of unlawful or restricted material. Stop once sufficient evidence exists to report the issue safely.
