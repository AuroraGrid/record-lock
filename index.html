RECORD LOCK

Controlled publication for claims that have to be inspected. Every claim is tied to sources, opposing views, confidence, and what would change the conclusion.

What you can examine
  • Claim schemas and revision conditions
  • Source provenance and evidence classification
  • Counterarguments and falsifiers
  • Public reference data only — no private case files
Working standard

Separate facts from inference. State what is unproven. Do not treat model confidence as accuracy. AI may draft; publication stays human-owned.
